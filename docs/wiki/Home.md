# Big Brother, week by week

A week-by-week grid of US Big Brother seasons 21–28, built from the "Voting history" tables
on Wikipedia. Each cell names who was evicted that week. Hovering or tapping a cell shows
the HOH, noms, veto winner(s), final noms, who was evicted, and the vote tally. The season
currently airing (BB28) is on top.
